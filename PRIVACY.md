# CapChecker privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

CapChecker has no server of its own. The developer (500altcoins) never receives your posts, your key, your usage or anything else.

## What stays on your device

- **Your Darkwoods API key**, in Chrome's local extension storage. It is never synced to your Google account and is only sent to Darkwoods.
- **Your settings** (sensitivity, models, daily cap), in Chrome's synced extension storage.
- **Saved results** for posts you've seen, for 7 days, so a post is never checked twice.
- **Today's usage counters** (credits spent, posts scanned and flagged), reset daily.

Pattern checks run entirely on your device.

## What leaves your device, and when

**Darkwoods (api.darkwoods.ai), only when an AI check runs.** CapChecker sends the post's text, the text of any quoted post or link card, the link addresses shown in the post, the author's handle and the post's address. The request is made with your API key and billed to your Darkwoods account. Darkwoods processes it under its own [privacy policy](https://darkwoods.ai). A deep check also lets the model search the web and fetch public pages through Darkwoods.

**fxtwitter (api.fxtwitter.com), only for deep checks, and only if "Fetch full post details" is on.** CapChecker sends the post's ID and author handle to this public service to read the author's public profile details and the full text of X Articles. You can turn this off in settings.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. CapChecker has no analytics, ads or tracking.

## Your control

- Turn CapChecker off from the toolbar popup or settings.
- Turn off auto-checks so AI checks only run when you click.
- Clear saved results in settings.
- Remove your key in settings, and revoke it in Darkwoods.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
